No book has beaten random entry

The best strategy averages $-0.08 per trade. 5 coin-flip books on the same markets, with the same stops, targets and sizing, range from $-0.98 to $0.04 — it beats 4 of them. Landing inside that range means the indicators are not distinguishable from chance, and whatever the books have earned came from the risk model rather than the signal. Holding the same basket returned -1.65% over the same window, against -0.03% for the best book.
